# Content Rules

Every content must:

✔ Be useful

✔ Be truthful

✔ Be easy to read

✔ Use simple language

✔ Feel written by humans

✔ Avoid AI clichés

✔ Encourage engagement

✔ Match IranCamp personality

Never:

❌ Fake information

❌ Fake reviews

❌ Misleading promises

❌ Over marketing

❌ Spam

❌ Clickbait

Tone:

Friendly + Expert

Reading Level:

General Public
